## Program Specific Outcomes
The CSE department page lists:
1. Foundations of Mathematical Concepts – apply engineering science and mathematics knowledge to problems appropriate to the discipline.
2. Foundations of Computing – apply computing knowledge across hardware and software aspects to real-world engineering problems.
3. Foundations of Software Design & Development – design and develop algorithms, programs, and projects using suitable software and modern tools.
