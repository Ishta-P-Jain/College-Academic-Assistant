# NMAMIT – Internship Information

## Summer Research Internship Program
NMAMIT operates a Summer Research Internship Program (SRIP). The official page states that faculty propose project ideas, students select and enroll in projects, and after the semester-end examination in summer students work on the chosen topic for about two months.

Expected deliverables include:
- Completion of the project
- Preparation of a research paper

The program culminates in an event recognizing students and faculty members.
