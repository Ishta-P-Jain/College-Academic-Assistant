# NMAMIT – Academic Calendar 2026–27

## Official calendar page
The official NMAMIT Academic Calendar page currently lists NDU 2026–27 calendars for:
- I and II Semester B.Tech
- III, V and VII Semester B.Tech
- IV and VI Semester B.Tech
- VIII Semester B.Tech
It also lists calendars for selected MCA and M.Tech batches and SEE timetables.

## Retrieval rule
Calendar questions are date-sensitive. Use the calendar applicable to the student's exact semester/year rather than giving a generic college-wide date.
