# NMAMIT CSE – Student FAQ Knowledge Base

## Academics
Q: Where can I find the CSE syllabus for my batch?
A: Use the official NMAMIT CSE department page. It currently lists NDU syllabus links including 2025–2029.

Q: Can an old regulation be used for my current batch?
A: Not automatically. Regulatory answers must use the rule or scheme applicable to the student's batch.

## Exams
Q: Where are examination procedures published?
A: The official NITTE examination portal publishes Examination Procedures & Guidelines, Results, Attendance & Marks, exam-related applications and timetables.

## Calendar
Q: Where can I find the academic calendar?
A: Use the official NMAMIT Academic Calendar page and select the calendar for the exact semester/year.

## Library
Q: What are the library timings?
A: The current NMAMIT Central Library page states that it is open daily from 7:30 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.

## Hostel
Q: Does NMAMIT have separate boys' and girls' hostels?
A: Yes. The official NMAMIT hostel page lists separate hostel blocks for boys and girls.

## Internship
Q: Does NMAMIT offer a summer research internship program?
A: Yes. NMAMIT's official Summer Research Internship Program provides research/project opportunities coordinated through faculty.

## Fees
Q: What is the 2026–27 annual tuition fee for B.Tech Computer Science at NMAMIT?
A: The current NUCAT page lists INR 3,50,000 standard fee, INR 2,00,000 scholarship fee and INR 4,00,000 NRI fee for the NMAMIT campus. Verify the academic year before answering.
