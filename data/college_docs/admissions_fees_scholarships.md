# NMAMIT – Admissions, Fees and Scholarships

## B.Tech admissions
The current NMAMIT admissions information provides admissions routes through NUCAT for applicable categories.

## NUCAT 2026–27 annual tuition fee – NMAMIT campus
The official NUCAT page currently lists for Computer Science:
- Standard Fee: INR 3,50,000
- Scholarship Fee: INR 2,00,000
- NRI Fee: INR 4,00,000

The same page notes an exam fee of INR 10,000 collected at the beginning of each academic year along with the annual tuition fee.

## NUCAT scholarships
The NMAMIT admissions page states that 20% of seats in each program/branch are set aside for candidates topping NUCAT, with scholarships of 20%–40% of the prescribed fee for those candidates.

## RAG rule
Fees are year-sensitive. Always mention the academic year used by the source.
