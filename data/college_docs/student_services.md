# NMAMIT – Student Services

## Central Library
The official NMAMIT Central Library page currently states:
- Open daily from 7:30 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.
- Located in the main wing of the academic building
- Capacity for more than 453 students simultaneously
- Automated using Koha and barcode-based circulation
- Digital Library facilities
- On-campus and remote access to electronic resources
- Resources include IEEE, Elsevier, Springer Nature, ProQuest, Scopus and Web of Science
- Turnitin plagiarism-detection service
- OPAC, question-paper archives, institutional repository, book-bank and other services

## Hostels
The official NMAMIT hostel page currently states:
- Separate hostel blocks for boys and girls
- 7 boys' hostel blocks
- 4 girls' hostel blocks
- Furnished rooms
- Vegetarian and non-vegetarian mess
- Campus Wi-Fi
- Health care and ambulance support
- RO drinking water
- Hot/cold water
- Power backup
- Laundry and security facilities
- Hostel code of conduct
