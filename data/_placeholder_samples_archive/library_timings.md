# Central Library

## Library Timings
The Central Library of the college is open on all working days during the semester.

- Monday to Friday: 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM
- Sunday and gazetted holidays: closed

During examination weeks the library extends its hours and remains open until
10:00 PM on weekdays. Students must carry their identity card to enter.

## Membership and Borrowing
- Each student may borrow up to 4 books for a period of 14 days.
- Books may be renewed twice if no other student has reserved them.
- Reference books, journals and question banks are for reading-room use only.
- A late return fine of Rs. 2 per day is charged per book.

## Services
The library subscribes to print journals, e-journals and DELNET membership.
A digital reading room with 40 terminals provides access to online databases.
