# Training & Placement Cell

## Placement Statistics
The Training and Placement cell coordinates campus recruitment for final year
students. Over 90 percent of eligible students receive offers each year.

## Recruiters
Regular recruiters include software product companies, core engineering
firms, banks and consulting agencies. Students attend written tests,
technical interviews and HR interviews conducted on campus.

## Pre-placement Training
The cell conducts aptitude classes, mock interviews and communication
workshops in the semester preceding placements.
