NMAM Institute of Technology (NMAMIT), Nitte
Summer Research Internship Program

Does NMAMIT offer a summer research internship program? Yes. NMAMIT offers a
summer research internship program every year for students who wish to gain
hands-on research experience during the summer vacation.

Duration: 6 to 8 weeks, typically from early May to mid June.

Eligibility: B.Tech students in their second, third or fourth year with a CGPA
of 7.0 or above, and M.Tech / M.Sc. students of the host institution.

Areas of research: artificial intelligence and machine learning, data science,
cyber security, VLSI design, structural engineering, biotechnology and
sustainable energy.

Stipend and support: selected interns receive a monthly stipend, laboratory
access and mentorship from faculty guides. A certificate is awarded on
successful completion of the internship.

How to apply: students apply through the Dean of Research by submitting a
statement of purpose and a letter of recommendation before 15 April.
