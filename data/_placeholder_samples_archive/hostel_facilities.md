# Hostel Life

## Hostel Facilities
On-campus hostels are available for both boys and girls, administered by the
Hostel Welfare Committee. The following hostel facilities are provided:

- Furnished rooms: single, double and triple sharing with study tables and cupboards
- 24x7 high-speed Wi-Fi internet in all hostel blocks
- Mess service: breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner, with vegetarian and
  non-vegetarian menus on rotation
- RO drinking water on every floor
- Laundry facility with washing machines on each floor
- Common room with television, indoor games and reading hall
- Gymnasium with basic fitness equipment
- Medical room with a visiting doctor and first-aid support
- 24x7 security, CCTV surveillance and biometric entry

## Hostel Rules
- Attendance is mandatory during week days; outings require warden permission.
- Visitors are allowed only in the designated reception area.
- Ragging in any form is a punishable offence.
